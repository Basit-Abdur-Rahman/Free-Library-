Free Little Family Library

Tiny Library big adventure

Introduction

The Free Little Family Library is the first of its kind in Peshawar – a community space where books, ideas, and people come together.

Timings

We welcome everyone, seven days a week, from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Borrowing Made Easy

Up to three books can be borrowed free of cost for one week. Our shelves hold around 10,000 books across a wide variety of subjects.

Our Collections

Science, Art, History, Geography, Politics, Pashto, Urdu, and English Literature, Languages, Textbooks, Maps & Atlases as well as a fascinating Children’s Section filled with stories and picture books

More Than A Library

Free Little Family Library is also a community hub. Space for lectures, discussions, and workshops Equipped with multi-media facilities

Supports audio and video recording for educational purposes

Whether you are a student, a researcher, a parent, or simply a lover of books, the library offers a place to learn, share, and grow together.
